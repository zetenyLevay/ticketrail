## About

**You order. You deliver.**

A personal task system that prints every task as a ticket on a thermal receipt printer, like orders on a kitchen rail.

It turns a simple to-do list into something more substantial, allowing you to capture a task quickly using the phone or desktop app. Each morning a list of to-dos will be automatically printed. Work your way through the paper slips one at a time and at the end close the shift and get a receipt of what has been done.

My main three criteria:
 - **The printer is a must**. Without it it is just a basic to-do app with a fancy receipt looking to-do list.
 - If a vault is connected then the notes are the source of truth.
 - The phone app should work without any add-ons keeping it fully modular
